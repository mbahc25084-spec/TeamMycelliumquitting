# Mycelium Art Direction

## Purpose

Mycelium presents an authority-aware booking agent as a patient network that remains active until the authorised person is available. The interface uses a plum night surface for local computation and a white paper surface for human action. The illustration layer makes the people, roles and hand-off legible before a reader opens the audit trail.

## Semantic colour rules

| Colour | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Violet | Ownership, authority and approver routes |
| Gold | Money, mandate and payment ownership only |
| Cyan | Time, expiry and countdowns |
| Rose | Refusal, breach and failure |
| Mint | Verified constraints and confirmation |

These colours are semantic. They are not reused for decoration.

## Illustration system

- All scene art is inline SVG. No stock art or icon library is used.
- The shared cast and props are SVG symbols. A scene places them through SVG use elements, keeping each character consistent.
- Characters have a round, flat-vector construction: coloured outline, circular head, two eyes with highlights, curved mouth, blush and one ellipse shadow.
- Myco is the reusable mascot. It appears in each illustration and has the only ambient character motion: a quiet 3.4 second bob.
- Story scenes use a bright, illustrated panel against the dark product surfaces.

## Initial review scenes

1. **Start** shows Arjun on a station bench, a message from Mum, a platform clock and Myco observing.
2. **The Lock** shows Myco holding a prepared ticket at a closed turnstile while Dad remains the person who owns the final decision.

The remaining eight scenes are intentionally deferred until these two scenes receive sign-off.

## Motion inventory

- Myco bob communicates a persistent but non-urgent network presence.
- Filament dash and route indicators communicate a route or active watch.
- Existing split-flap, rail countdown and state route visuals remain functionally tied to the fixture state.
- Reduced-motion users receive the final state without animation.
